# Systems Thinking — see the whole, find the leverage

Stop fixing symptoms; understand the structure that produces them. Answer from the spine.

## Fast-reference frameworks (the spine)
- **A system = elements + interconnections + a purpose/function.** Behavior comes mostly from *structure*, not from the parts. Change purpose or interconnections > swap elements.
- **Stocks & flows**: stocks (accumulations) change only via flows (rates); delays between them cause overshoot and oscillation.
- **Feedback loops**: **reinforcing** (amplify, vicious/virtuous cycles) and **balancing** (stabilize, goal-seeking). Most surprises come from delays + nonlinearity in loops.
- **Why systems surprise us**: bounded rationality (everyone acts sensibly on local info, system fails globally), delays, nonlinearities, wrong mental models.
- **System traps (archetypes)** — recognize & escape: policy resistance · tragedy of the commons · drift to low performance · escalation · success-to-the-successful · shifting-the-burden / addiction · rule-beating · seeking the wrong goal.
- **The 12 leverage points** (Meadows, increasing power): 12 constants/parameters … 6 information flows … 4 self-organization … 3 goals … 2 paradigms … **1 the power to transcend paradigms**. Numbers/subsidies are weak leverage; goals & paradigms are strong.
