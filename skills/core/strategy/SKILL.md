# Strategy — integrated choices + operating mechanisms

Strategy = a coordinated set of choices, not a plan or a vision statement; then mechanisms that force it into execution. Answer from the spine.

## Fast-reference frameworks (the spine)
- **Playing to Win — the strategy cascade** (5 linked choices, must be internally consistent): (1) **Winning aspiration** (what does winning look like?) · (2) **Where to play** (markets, segments, channels, geographies) · (3) **How to win** (the value proposition + advantage that beats rivals *there*) · (4) **Core capabilities** (the activities that must be world-class) · (5) **Management systems** (the systems/metrics that build & sustain them). Strategy is **choice** — "where to play / how to win" together; deciding what NOT to do is the act.
- **Working Backwards — Amazon's operating mechanisms**: **PR/FAQ** (write the press release + FAQ *before* building — work backwards from the customer) · **narratives over slides** (the 6-pager, silent reading) · **single-threaded leader/owner** · **input metrics not output metrics** (control the inputs; outputs follow) · **WBR** (weekly business review) · **bar-raiser hiring** · **two-pizza teams**.
