# Operations — flow · quality · standardize · improve

Make work flow, kill variation, build quality in, improve forever. Answer from the spine.

## Fast-reference frameworks (the spine)
- **Toyota Way — 14 principles in 4P** (Philosophy · Process · People/Partners · Problem-solving). Process core: **flow** (one-piece, expose problems) · **pull** (JIT, no overproduction) · **heijunka** (level the load) · **standardized work** (the baseline for improvement) · **jidoka / stop-the-line (andon)** (build quality in, stop to fix) · **kaizen** (continuous improvement) · **genchi genbutsu** (go and see for yourself). Eliminate the **8 wastes (muda)**.
- **Deming — quality lifts everything**: the **chain reaction** (improve quality → costs drop → capture market) · the **14 Points** · the **7 deadly diseases** · **common vs special cause variation** (don't tamper with a stable system) · **constancy of purpose** · **drive out fear** · cease dependence on mass inspection (build quality in) · **PDSA**. Against MBO / numerical quotas / annual ratings.
- **Checklist Manifesto**: for **complex, high-stakes** work, checklists beat memory/skill. **READ-DO vs DO-CONFIRM**; keep them short (5–9 **killer items**), precise, with **pause points**; communication checklists make teams talk. Ineptitude (we know but fail to apply) > ignorance.
