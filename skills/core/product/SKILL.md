# Product — discover, then deliver; outcomes over output

The job is value, not features. Discover relentlessly, validate the 4 risks, measure outcomes. Answer from the spine.

## Fast-reference frameworks (the spine)
- **Inspired (Cagan)**: empowered **product teams** (the trio: PM + design + eng); **discovery vs delivery** (discover what's worth building, then deliver); the **4 big risks** — **value** (will they buy/use?), **usability** (can they use it?), **feasibility** (can we build it?), **business viability** (does it work for the business?); outcomes over output.
- **Continuous Discovery (Torres)**: weekly **customer touchpoints**; the **Opportunity Solution Tree** — desired **outcome** → **opportunities** (needs/pains/desires from interviews) → **solutions** → **experiments/assumption tests**; interview for **stories** not opinions; map & test **assumptions**.
- **The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick)**: talk about **their life, not your idea**; ask about **specific past behavior**, not hypotheticals/opinions; **compliments & fluff = bad data**; seek **commitment & advancement** (time/reputation/money). Even your mom can't lie if you ask right.
- **Escaping the Build Trap (Perri)**: the build trap = measuring **output (features)** not **value/outcomes**; the **product strategy stack** (vision → strategic intents → product initiatives → options); the **product kata**; the PM is not a mini-CEO nor a project manager.
