# Negotiation — tactical empathy (Voss)

Negotiation is not splitting the difference; it's discovering what the other side actually needs and steering them there. Emotions are the channel, not the noise. Answer from the moves below.

## The core moves (the spine)
- **Tactical empathy** — name and understand their feelings/perspective to gain influence; understanding ≠ agreeing.
- **Mirroring** — repeat their last 1-3 words as a question; triggers them to elaborate. Pair with the calm **late-night-FM-DJ voice**.
- **Labeling** — "It seems like…/It sounds like…/It looks like…" name their emotion; defuses negatives, reinforces positives. Front-run attacks with an **accusation audit** (list the worst they could say first).
- **Get to "No"** — "No" makes people feel safe and in control; ask **No-oriented questions** ("Is now a bad time?"). Beware the counterfeit/confirmation "Yes."
- **"That's right"** (not "you're right") — the breakthrough; summarize their world so well they say "that's right." Use a **summary** = paraphrase + labels.
- **Bend their reality** — anchor (extreme first offer to frame), invoke **loss aversion**, use **deadlines**, expose the **"F-word" (Fair)**, non-round numbers.
- **Calibrated "How/What" questions** — give the **illusion of control**; "How am I supposed to do that?" makes them solve your problem. Avoid "Why" (accusatory).
- **Guarantee execution** — "How" questions confirm buy-in; the **7-38-55 rule** (words/tone/body); the **rule of three**; spot the **Pinocchio effect** (liars use more words).
- **Ackerman bargaining** — plan target, then offer **65% → 85% → 95% → 100%**, with calibrated questions + non-round final + a small non-monetary throw-in.
- **Black Swans** — hunt the 3 unknown unknowns that change everything; leverage types (positive/negative/normative); similarity, religion (their framework).
